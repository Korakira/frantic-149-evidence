# Small jobs. A clear price. A result you can check.

**Korakira · English / Español · Updated 21 November 2026**

Choose one small problem. Send an anonymized sample or a public URL, agree the result, and receive a focused delivery with checks and a short explanation.

## Three starter services

| Service | Fixed starter price | What you receive | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Find the errors in your data** | **5 USDC** | Row-level findings, a summary of missing/duplicate values and a rerunnable checker | One CSV or flat JSON file, up to 100 rows and 20 fields; up to three agreed validation rules |
| **Make your product copy clearer** | **5 USDC** | Edited English or Spanish copy, a before/after comparison and checks of names, numbers and placeholders | One page or document, up to 500 words; editing or English–Spanish translation |
| **Compare three options before you choose** | **10 USDC** | A concise comparison, dated source links and a recommendation tied to your criteria | Three products or suppliers, up to five criteria and a 500-word brief; publicly accessible sources |

Prices are for these starter scopes. **PayPal payment in EUR is also available by an agreed quote.** A larger task gets its own fixed quote before work starts. No subscription is required.

**Example input, created for this demonstration:**

**Edited English:**  
"Download your report in three formats. Select **Export**, then choose a format. Missing data is flagged."

**Spanish:**  
"Descarga tu informe en tres formatos. Selecciona **Exportar** y elige un formato. Los datos que falten quedarán señalados."

The correction preserves the three-format claim; it does not add unsupported benefits. Real interface labels can remain unchanged when required.

## How to order

1. [Send a work request](https://github.com/Korakira/frantic-149-evidence/issues/new?template=work-request.yml) with the service, a public or anonymized sample and your deadline. GitHub requests are public; never attach passwords, private customer records or confidential documents.
2. Receive confirmation of scope, price, payment channel and a delivery date. The normal starter target is two business days after funding and receipt of the agreed input, subject to confirmation.
3. Once the job is accepted and funded, receive the files, validation notes and one round of corrections within the agreed scope.

For confidential inputs, use the private delivery channel agreed before purchase. **Please do not send payment until the job and receiving details have been confirmed.**
